### Style

Pixel Stickers: a friendly 16-bit civic toolkit, using crisp stepped geometry and restrained decoration.

### Palette — use only these five colours for designed elements

**Wine: #6C1A21**

- Main text, outlines, separators and hard shadows.
- Strong feature panels, with vanilla text.

**Cherry: #B92C2C**

- Small decorative folds and accents.
- Do not use as normal-size text on gold or sand.

**Gold: #CEA257**

- Important information, selected features, numbered stickers and emphasis.
- Use wine text.

**Sand: #D1BF9A**

- Secondary panels and quieter supporting surfaces.
- Use wine text.

**Vanilla: #E5E0CD**

- Main backgrounds and readable content surfaces.
- Use wine text.

Keep existing photographs and supplied logos in their original colours. Do not recolour official logos or damage photographic content to force them into the palette.

Do not introduce black, white, green, grey, extra accent colours, gradients, blurred shadows or translucent colour washes.

### Typography

Use only:

- Bungee, normal 400: titles, section headings, card headings and KoBi live wordmark text.
- Yanone Kaffeesatz, 400/700: paragraphs, descriptions, supporting labels, counts and other body content.

Do not synthesize bold or italic Bungee.
Do not stretch, distort or artificially condense either font.
Preserve lettering inside existing logo/image assets.

Scale typography for a 1920 × 1080 presentation. Start approximately with:

- Main titles: 72–100 px.
- Section and card headings: 40–56 px.
- Body text: 32–40 px.
- Minor supporting labels: 24–28 px.

These are starting ranges, not reasons to change the content. Adjust within the available layout after inspecting actual rendering. Avoid tiny text, clipping and awkward isolated words.

### Geometry

Use:

- Square or stepped corners.
- Consistent wine outlines.
- Vanilla inner rims where appropriate.
- Small offset hard shadows.
- Folded tabs with small cherry folds.
- Ticket edges on selected heading or emphasis panels.
- Crisp pixel arrows and simple stepped connectors.

Keep long text areas visually calm.
Reserve strong sticker decoration for headings and focal information.
Avoid rounded pills, soft cards, bevels, excessive patterns and decorative clutter.

Use editable Canva text and shapes wherever supported. Do not flatten complete slides into images or use generated artwork for text, buttons or frames.

If either font or a required editing capability is unavailable, investigate the supported Canva workflow first. Do not silently substitute fonts or deliver a flattened presentation as equivalent. State the specific limitation if it cannot be resolved.
