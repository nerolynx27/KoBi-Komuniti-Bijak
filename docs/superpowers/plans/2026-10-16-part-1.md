## Review focus

- Corrupt or future storage must remain byte-for-byte intact before explicit reset.
- Failed writes/reset cannot announce success or mutate committed records.
- Loading action and keyboard activation cannot repeat an operation.
- Modal focus restoration and confirmation cancellation must preserve state.
- Text enlargement cannot crop labels, focus or introduce horizontal scrolling.
